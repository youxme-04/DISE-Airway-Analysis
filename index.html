Geometric Rationale

รอการวิเคราะห์...

Clinical Analytics Pipeline (Research Prototype)

Designed with state-of-the-art computer vision to translate subjective video signals into objective geometric features.

Automated Segmentation

Morphological dynamic contour tracking and median filtering ensure clean, noise-free boundaries of the upper airway lumen.

3D Mesh Modeling

A dynamic three-dimensional mesh represents the patient's airway envelope, adapting automatically to real-time contraction data.

Aspect Ratio & Angles

Fitting equations calculate the aspect ratios and major axis tilt angles of the airway shape to categorize collapse patterns.

Standardized Reports

Generates print-ready geometric summaries containing quantified values, reference ranges, and visual patient assets.
